Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

Maggie's Magic Mirror & Makeover ("Maggie's Magic Mirror", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the Maggie's Magic Mirror & Makeover mobile application ("App").

By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect the following information:

Personal Information

  • Name
  • Mobile phone number
  • Date of birth
  • Profile photograph
  • Skin type
  • Allergy information that you choose to provide

Booking Information

When you make or manage an appointment, we may collect:

  • Selected services or packages
  • Appointment date and time
  • Booking status
  • Booking history
  • Rescheduling or cancellation information
  • Information associated with completed appointments

Reviews and Feedback

If you choose to submit a review or feedback, we may collect:

  • Star rating
  • Written feedback
  • Information associated with the booking being reviewed

Device and Notification Information

If you enable notifications, the App may use a device notification token to send relevant notifications, such as:

  • Booking confirmations
  • Booking updates
  • Appointment-related notifications
  • Service completion notifications
  • Other important App notifications

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to provide and improve the services offered through the App, including:

  • Creating and maintaining your customer profile
  • Verifying your mobile phone number
  • Managing appointments and bookings
  • Showing your booking history
  • Managing rescheduling and cancellations
  • Processing and displaying reviews and feedback
  • Sending appointment and service-related notifications
  • Personalizing relevant customer experiences
  • Sending occasional birthday or occasion greetings when appropriate
  • Maintaining the security and proper operation of the App
  • Responding to customer requests and support needs

We do not require you to make a booking in order to receive an occasion or birthday greeting when such a feature is used.

3. Profile Photographs

If you choose to add a profile photograph, the photograph is uploaded to our cloud storage service and associated with your customer profile.

Providing a profile photograph is optional.

You can choose not to provide a profile photograph without preventing you from using the core booking functionality of the App.

4. Sensitive Information You Choose to Provide

The App may allow you to provide information such as allergies and skin type.

This information is optional and is provided by you to help Maggie's Magic Mirror understand relevant service preferences or considerations.

Please do not provide information that is not necessary for the intended purpose of the App.

5. Firebase and Service Providers

The App uses Google Firebase services to support functionality such as:

  • Phone authentication
  • Cloud Firestore database services
  • Cloud Storage
  • Cloud Functions
  • Push notifications

These services may process information on our behalf as necessary to provide the App's functionality.

Google's services are subject to Google's applicable privacy and security practices.

6. Notifications

If notification permissions are enabled on your device, we may send notifications related to your bookings, appointments, service completion, reviews, and other relevant App activity.

You can manage notification permissions through your device settings.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the App against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App's services, maintain booking and customer records, handle reviews and feedback, comply with applicable requirements, and resolve disputes or support requests.

When information is no longer required for these purposes, it may be deleted or otherwise removed in accordance with our applicable data-management practices.

9. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including the ability to:

  • Request access to information associated with your account
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account or personal information, subject to applicable legal or legitimate business requirements
  • Withdraw optional information such as a profile photograph
  • Manage notification permissions through your device

To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the contact details provided below.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for general customers of Maggie's Magic Mirror & Makeover and is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly request personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us improperly, please contact us so that we can review the request.

11. Third-Party Services

The App may rely on third-party services, including Google Firebase, to provide authentication, cloud storage, database, cloud functions, and notification functionality.

Those services may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

When changes are made, the updated Privacy Policy will be published at the same public location and the Effective Date will be updated.
